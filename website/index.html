01 THE LORE

The Shattering of Antrom

Antrom was a kingdom once — a whole world of crystal veins and plasma seas, ruled from a crown of mountains at its heart. Then the veins woke up. The aether crystal that powered the kingdom turned out to be something older than the kingdom itself, and when the deep drills bit too far, the Crown Mountains shattered and the world cracked with them.

What is left are battlegrounds: a scarred moonscape where the Crown once stood, and archipelagos of green islands scattered across a rising ocean. Every fragment is rich with the two things worth fighting for — aether crystal and plasma — and three powers have come to claim them.

Three Banners Over the Ruins

The Vanguard are the exiled colonists of old Antrom — engineers and soldiers who lost their kingdom and intend to rebuild it one command post at a time. Their crews weld, their fleets hold the line, and their Warmaster leads from the front.

The Aethyr were here first. The crystal is not a resource to them — it is their ancestors. Their gilded war-machines assemble themselves from light, and their Riftlord does not forgive the drills that woke the veins.

The Myriad are what grew in the cracks the Shattering left behind: a living horde with one will and endless bodies. They do not build. They spawn. And every battlefield they touch becomes a nest.

The War for the Veins

No treaty survives contact with a fresh crystal field. Commanders drop onto the fragments of Antrom, raise a base from nothing, and fight until every enemy banner is ash — the only victory the veins recognize is a battlefield with a single flag on it. Harvest the crystal, tap the plasma vents, climb the tech tree, and choose your moment: the war for Antrom is won by the commander who turns resources into an army one push faster than everyone else.

04 THE BATTLEGROUNDS

SHATTERED CROWN

The classic. One landmass, six starts, nowhere to hide.

The scarred plateau where the Crown Mountains fell. Solid ground from edge to edge means every army walks to war — expansions are contested on foot and the rich gold veins in the center belong to whoever dares hold the middle.

ISLANDS

No land routes. The ocean decides who lives.

Square isles scattered across open water. Ground armies are stranded without transports, flyers rule the skies, and the empty isles between the starts are free real estate for anyone bold enough to ferry a colony crew across the straits.

05 HOW TO PLAY

1. Jump in

Open the plot in Decentraland — no install, it runs in your browser as a Decentraland scene.

2. Pick your war

Single player against up to five AI commanders, or multiplayer battle rooms against real people.

3. Boom

Train workers, harvest crystal, tap plasma vents, and climb the tech tree toward titans and capital ships.

4. Bust

Raze every enemy structure on the map. Last banner standing wins Antrom.
